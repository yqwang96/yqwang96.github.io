## Research overview

Solar radiation varies with cloud cover, the position of the sun, and local geography. Coarse weather grids provide useful regional information, while applications such as solar-energy assessment need a more detailed view of its spatial variation.

This ongoing research explores how physical knowledge and deep learning can work together to reconstruct finer-scale hourly radiation fields. The approach uses a clear-sky background as a reference, learns local spatial variation, and examines how calibration responds to changing solar and cloud conditions.

## What I am investigating

The central question is whether these physical cues help a model represent both radiation values and spatial patterns. I study their contribution through controlled comparisons, paying attention to cases where a lower numerical error does not bring a better spatial reconstruction.

The work currently evaluates agreement with a higher-resolution reference weather product. Independent ground observations would be needed to establish accuracy against measured surface radiation. Experiments and manuscript preparation are ongoing.
